Legacy Since 1896

Sports Committee History

Founded in 1896, the Boğaziçi University Sports Committee has grown into one of Europe's premier student-led sports organizations.

For decades, Sports Fest has hosted tens of thousands of student-athletes worldwide on the historic South Campus overlooking the Bosphorus Strait.

Visit the Sports Committee Website
1896

The Festival Timeline

  • 1896: Official founding of the Boğaziçi Sports Committee.
  • 1980: The first official Sports Fest organized by the Sports Committee, expanding beyond borders to welcome universities across Europe.
  • Today: Hosting over 1,000+ athletes annually across 12 disciplines.
